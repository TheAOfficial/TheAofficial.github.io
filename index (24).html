I'm an Ethical Hacker

I'm not just dreaming about technology; I'm preparing to shape its future.

I'm an Ethical Hacker, passionate about Cybersecurity, Programming, Artificial Intelligence, and Advanced Technology. I like to understand how systems really work: how software is built, where it can break, and how it can be made safer for everyone who depends on it.

My vision is to master the art of ethical hacking, understand digital security, discover vulnerabilities, and protect the digital world. I believe the best defenders are the ones who think like attackers, but always act with permission, responsibility, and respect for the law.
